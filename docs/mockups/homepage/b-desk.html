Context-aware controller

Your phone becomes the buttons for whatever's on screen.

deckd watches which app — or website — is focused on your computer and hands the right buttons to any browser on your network.

Install Source
Web apps

Websites get their own decks.

Other remotes see "Firefox" and stop there. deckd reads the window title, so Gmail, YouTube and Meet each get their own buttons — driving the site's own keyboard shortcuts. No browser extension.

Also shipped: Netflix. Any site with keyboard shortcuts can have one.

Media

Every player, one list.

A music app, a podcast in a browser tab, a video in mpv — whatever is playing shows up here with play, pause and skip. It's one tap away from any deck.

Trackpad

The phone is the mouse.

For whatever a deck doesn't cover: drag to move, tap to click, and a keyboard for typing a URL from the couch.

Scroll strip

A jog wheel on every deck.

The strip on the right scrolls the focused window. Flick it and it coasts.

Decks
Picked by app or by website. Build them in the in-browser editor; every screen updates as you save.
Security
Localhost-only by default. Shared password for LAN clients. Input refused while the session is locked.
